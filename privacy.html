Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

SkyWatch is built from the ground up with a strict Zero Digital Footprint & Zero Knowledge Privacy Architecture. Your privacy and security are completely protected by mathematical encryption.

1. Zero Log & Zero Knowledge Guarantee

We do not collect, store, track, or log any personal information, phone numbers, email addresses, IP addresses, or device identifiers. We have no user registration databases, no telemetry, and no tracking scripts.

2. Client-Side End-to-End Encryption

All private messages are encrypted directly on your local device using 256-bit AES-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations) prior to transmission. Unencrypted plain text never leaves your device and is never readable by server operators, third parties, or internet service providers.

3. Local Data Storage

Your PIN codes, decoy photos, and secret phrase room settings are hashed or stored exclusively in your local web browser storage (localStorage). This data remains on your physical device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache or pressing Clear Chat inside the app.

4. Location & Weather Forecast Data

To display weather forecast information, SkyWatch queries public weather APIs (Open-Meteo). Approximate location coordinates (latitude and longitude) are processed locally on your device or defaulted to Sullivan. Location coordinates are never linked to your identity or stored on remote servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Encrypted message payloads (containing only unreadable ciphertext and random initialization vectors) are relayed through Firebase Realtime Database and local sync broadcast channels for real-time delivery. Firebase cannot decrypt your messages as the encryption keys exist only on your device.

6. Contact & Inquiries

Because SkyWatch operates on a zero-knowledge architecture, we do not hold user accounts or personal records. For questions regarding source code and technical implementation, refer to the open project documentation.
